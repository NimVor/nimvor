# Code of Conduct

WhaCP is built by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
We want every contributor to feel welcome and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback about code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers; explain the "why".
- Credit the work of others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat)
and when representing the project elsewhere. Report problems privately to the
maintainers via the contact on the repository owner's GitHub profile.
Maintainers will review every report confidentially and may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban participants who violate this code.
